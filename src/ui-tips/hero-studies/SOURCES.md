## What is reconstructed
Five HTML/CSS blueprint diagrams, semantic readable headings/copy, navigation representations, product information, buttons represented within the visual studies, CSS light traces and arc, custom SVG ZYRO lettering, comparison controls and original learning annotations. Each desktop composition is drawn at a fixed 1000 px width and proportionally scaled through ResizeObserver; the four-column and organic arrangements remain intact on a phone rather than being invented as a different mobile site.

1. Stepped black platform: centred title, descending blocks, line highlights, white action, partner-style marks. The source cuts away while its second line is still typing: only “not” is visible in the last frame. The recreation shows “not…” and does not invent a complete sentence.
2. Future arm prosthetics: central diagonal prosthetic elements, technical three-line title, media/article edge panels. The source visibly spells “WHIT AI”; it is retained. A standard heavy face approximates the unidentified original display font. Logo and tiny supporting details remain approximations.
3. Yeezy: pink/petal product field, glass-like top controls, bottom-right YEEZY BOOST 350 V2 CLAY, $299,99, PRE-ORDER cluster. Source shoe footage preserves the moving product/petals.
4. Minipresso GR2: white upper-left copy area, connected organic video mask, hand-held thumbnail, brewing/product imagery, SHOP and NEW COLLECTION. The exact mask curves and small wordmark differ slightly.
5. ZYRO: black grid, glowing blue-white semicircular arc, custom SVG wordmark, Everything in One Place, Download Demo and Learn More. Arc and wordmark motion are recreated in CSS; tiny footer details are approximated.

## Derived imagery and motion
`public/hero-studies/` contains short silent H.264 crops and JPEG posters/details extracted from the user-supplied reel. These contain the original visual material; they are not independently rendered 3D assets. Footage is masked within reconstructed page compositions, not used as a full reel embed. Prosthetic clip ≈4.48–5.00 s, Yeezy ≈5.72–6.28 s, Minipresso ≈6.88–7.86 s. Short excerpts loop and may show a visible seam. Small inset images are also crops. No full original reel or presenter/audio is included in the app.

The reel does not provide an open-source code or asset license. This is an attributed local learning recreation, not a claim of ownership or a licensed template pack. No deployment was performed for this task.

## Interactions and accessibility
Five gallery cards open native modal dialogs. Blueprint/Finished/Compare, replay/pause, native range comparison (arrow keys/Home/End), three numbered analysis notes linked to decorative sketch marks, Previous/Next, original reel link, Escape close/focus restoration. Default autoplay holds blueprint 3.5 s and finished 8 s. Media and timeline pause in hidden documents; reduced-motion preference disables autoplay/entrances. Gallery footage is paused. Manual final views show completed text even when motion is paused. Scene buttons are visual study elements; they do not place orders or submit forms. Teaching prose is our analysis, not a transcript of the creator.
